# 常用替换

important → essential / vital / pivotal / crucial

like → be into / be a big fan of / be keen on

don't like → not be my thing / not be my cup of tea

I prefer → I'd go for / I'd pick / ... works better for me

I think → I'd say / I would say

vibe → atmosphere

stumble upon → come across

# 使用方式

1. 根据题目选活动与氛围，不要把全部词塞进一个答案。
2. 只保留能自然说出的表达，每次尝试替换两三处。
