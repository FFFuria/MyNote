## 词汇多样性 LR

### 改述 paraphrase

*课堂改述示例：尝试用具体感受说明“宽敞”，不要只反复说 spacious。*

### 代词 pronouns

- 人名 → he / she / this person

## 语法多样性与准确性 GRA

*先保证时态准确，再灵活增加句型。*

- 日常习惯：一般现在时。
- 过去经历：一般过去时。
- 持续至今：现在完成时 / 现在完成进行时。
- 背景动作：过去进行时。
- 未来计划：将来时。
- 适合时可以使用现在进行时或虚拟结构，不要为凑句型而硬加。

> I've been living here for ... years.
> I've been studying at this university for ... years.

### which 与 where
- **which**：指代地点，在从句里作主语或宾语。
- **where**：表示“在这个地方”，相当于 in which。

> Guangzhou, which is located in the south of China, ...
> I love this classroom where I can study.

### 时态错误提醒

课堂错误示例：
> ❌ I want to ate in a Japanese restaurant. I didn't go there before, so I wanted to tried it.

整理后的示例：

> ✅ I wanted to eat at a Japanese restaurant last Friday. I'd never been there before, so I decided to give it a try.

# Part 1 答题方式

## 核心结构

> **直接回答 + 拓展**

拓展选取适合的两三种逻辑，不必每题全部使用：

| 逻辑 | 表达 |
| --- | --- |
| 原因 | because / since / as / The main reason is ... |
| 解释 | I mean ... / What I mean is ... |
| 例子 | for example / for instance / especially / like / such as |
| 感受 | I feel like ... / It makes me feel ... |
| 评价 | I think ... / I would say ... / ..., which is really + adj. |
| 补充 | Plus / Besides / On top of that / What's more |
| 转折与对比 | But / That said / However / Compared with ... |
| 结果 | So / As a result / Therefore |
| 条件 | If / When / Once / As long as / Unless |

*课堂衔接词表还有 moreover、furthermore、thus、hence、consequently 等；练口语时按语境选择自然的表达。*

### 衔接词语法

- because / since / as + **句子**。
- because of / due to / owing to / thanks to + **名词或名词短语**。
- although / though / while / whereas + **句子**。
- in spite of + **名词或动名词**。
- like / such as / including + **举例对象**。

## 特殊疑问句 5W1H

*问什么先回答什么。*

### Where do you come from?

> **地点 + 地理位置 + 形容词 + 有什么 / 评价**

- 地点：Shenzhen / Guangzhou / 自己的家乡。
- 方位：which is located in the south of China。
- 描述：modern / liveable / an international metropolis。
- 补充：there is / there are ...。

## 一般疑问句 Yes or No

### 肯定回答

- Yeah, definitely.
- For sure.
- I'd say so.
- Absolutely.

### 否定回答

- Not really.
- I wouldn't say so.
- I'm afraid not.

### Is there anything that you dislike about your hometown?

*这里 Yes 表示“确实有不喜欢的地方”，注意对应问题的含义。*

> Yes. I don't really like the weather because it rains a lot, which is kinda annoying.

- 天气：经常下雨 → 外出需要带伞 → annoying。
- 交通：not so convenient → I mean ... → 与朋友出去花很多时间 → exhausting。

## 选择疑问句

*直接选择，不需要先说 Yes 或 No。*

- I'd go for A. The main reason is ...
- Compared with B, A just works better for me.
- I'd pick B, mainly because ...
- A is not really my thing.
- So that's my pick.
- At the end of the day, B wins.

## 常见问题提醒

- Do you live in a house or an apartment? → 先回答住宿类型。
- What do you like about the area where you live? → 回答喜欢什么，不能只介绍城市历史。
- Do you often visit parks in your city? → 先回答频率，再解释或举例。

## Street markets

### Are there many street markets in China?

结构：直接回答 → 例子 → 原因 → 感受或评价。

> Yeah, quite a few of them. You can stumble upon them pretty much everywhere, especially in popular scenic spots like ancient towns. People go there not only for bargain hunting but also to pick up souvenirs. I'd say it's usually a pleasant experience, since they can just wander around and soak in that bustling vibe.

关键词：ancient towns → bargain hunting → souvenirs → wander around → bustling vibe。

### Do you prefer to go shopping in a shopping mall or at a street market?

结构：选择 → 原因 → 补充原因 → 不同场景下的转折。

> Well, if it's for everyday shopping, I'll definitely go for shopping centres since they're more comfortable and air-conditioned. Plus, I don't have to haggle for lower prices. That said, when I'm in another city travelling, I absolutely enjoy wandering around street markets to enjoy the bustling atmosphere.

关键词：everyday shopping → mall → comfortable / air-conditioned → travelling → street market。

## 地点类通用要点

1. 地理位置：which is located in / lies in / is situated in ...。
2. 有什么：there is / there are ...。
3. 喜欢的原因：**活动 + 氛围**。

# Part 2 答题方式

## 题型分类

| 类型 | 课堂例题 |
| --- | --- |
| 人物 | a popular or well-known person in your country；a helpful person |
| 物品 | a painting that you like；a photo that makes you happy |
| 地点 | an interesting place in your country；a place you would like to visit in the future |
| 事件与经历 | an occasion when you received incorrect information；a childhood experience you enjoyed |

## 审题三要素

1. **描述对象**：a place / a person / an experience ...。
2. **限定条件**：in your country / interesting / have travelled to / would recommend ...。
3. **提示点与时态**：看清 What / Where / When / Who / Why，区分过去经历和当前描述。

*课堂练习目标时长：1 分 45 秒—2 分钟。四个提示点有轻有重，用细节支撑核心内容。*

## 地点类结构

### 背景

- 开头：对象 + 限定条件改述 + 地理位置。
- 时因地人：什么时候、为什么、在哪里、和谁。
- 城市简述：1—2 句话。

> I'm going to tell you about ...
> Well, the city I've visited and highly recommend is definitely Guangzhou, which is located in the south of China.
> I'd say it's a modern and liveable city.

### 主体

> **衔接句 + 活动一及细节 + 活动二及细节 + 感受 / 评价**

- During our stay, we did a ton of fascinating things, so I'd recommend it to people who are nature lovers. What I mean is, ...
- I highly recommend this place mainly because there are so many things to do and enjoy. To be specific, ...
- On top of that, ...

拓展活动、地点形容词和画面感：[[口语——地点类语料#活动]]。

### 结尾

- So / Overall / All in all ...。
- 回扣题目：为什么推荐 / 为什么想再去。
- 可以自然补充未来计划。

> In the future, I'll definitely go there again.

## 推荐旅行过的地方

### 话题卡

Describe a place you have travelled to that you would like to recommend to others.

You should say:

- What it is
- Where it is
- What you saw and did there
- And explain why you would like to recommend it to others

### 阿勒泰素材骨架

*这是课堂示例，练习时替换成自己的真实经历。*

| 环节 | 关键词 |
| --- | --- |
| 地点与方位 | Altay；north-western China；inland / landlocked |
| 时间与原因 | winter；December to February；freezing but magical；snow-covered |
| 同行者 | close friends；adventurous；photography；winter sports |
| 景观与文化 | snow-capped mountains；wooden cabins；ancient villages；local folk culture |
| 活动一 | wake up at 6 a.m.；crunch through thick snow；catch the sunrise |
| 活动二 | iconic landmarks / local food / winter festivals，按自己的经历选取 |
| 感受 | serene；picturesque；fairy tale；breathtaking winter wonderland |
| 推荐原因 | scenery；activities；a getaway from the city |

### 主体画面感示例

> I remember one winter, we went to an ancient village in Altay. That day, we actually woke up around 6 a.m. and crunched through the thick snow just to catch the sunrise. And honestly, the best part was watching the sunlight slowly hit the distant snow-capped mountains. Below them, there was smoke curling up from the cosy wooden cabins in the village. It felt like we were in a fairy tale. The whole view was absolutely serene and picturesque.

### 结尾示例

> So yeah, looking back, I honestly don't think I've ever seen such a breathtaking winter wonderland. It was definitely the most fascinating trip I've taken with my adventurous friends.
>
> Even now, whenever I look at those photos, it brings back that serene and cosy feeling. To me, it remains a truly picturesque getaway and a perfect escape from the bustling urban jungle.

*用来学习组织方式和表达，练习时只看关键词，现场展开。*
